Offshore VR - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Offshore VR ("the game") is a multiplayer VR fishing game for Meta Quest, made by Trash Game Studio, Germany ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, who handles it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Data we collect

  • Meta account identifier. When you sign in with your Meta account, we receive your Meta user ID and a one-time proof from Meta that confirms it is you. We use it only to give you a game account. We do not receive your name, email, friends list or other profile details.
  • Game account ID. A player ID created by Unity Gaming Services. If Meta sign-in is not available, the game creates an anonymous Unity account instead.
  • Game progress. Gold, caught fish, bait, boats, settings, and anti-cheat information such as catch timing.
  • Public profile. The username shown above your character and on leaderboards, and a verified badge if we gave you one.
  • Multiplayer and voice. Room and session information so players can join each other. Voice chat audio is sent in real time through Unity Vivox to the other players in your room. We do not record or store it.
  • Moderation. If you are banned, we store that your account is banned.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, payment information or advertising identifiers, and we do not show ads.

2. Why we use it

To sign you in, save your progress, run leaderboards and multiplayer, stop cheating, and moderate players.

3. Who handles the data

  • Meta Platforms - provides the sign-in on Quest.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Multiplayer, Vivox) - stores your game data and runs the game server.
  • Cloudflare and a small server we operate - pass the Meta sign-in check between the game and Unity.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. Data may be processed in the United States and in the EU/EEA by these providers.

4. How long we keep it

Game data is kept while your account exists. We delete it when you ask us to (see below).

5. Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and all stored data, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing trashvrgames@gmail.com with your username. We will respond within 30 days. You can also stop playing at any time.

6. Age

The game is for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
